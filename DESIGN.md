# Design System: Mikro

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "A quiet gallery with dot-matrix character"**

A quiet photo gallery with dot-matrix character: black, chalk white and muted rust, with a cream light appearance. The user-pinned mockup and gallery reference define the visual direction. Photos remain the densest part of the screen; controls use familiar Flutter navigation and confirmation patterns.

This records the built Flutter UI, not a complete V2 specification. Source: `lib/ui/theme.dart`, `lib/ui/app.dart`, `lib/ui/albums.dart`, and the visual contract in `lib/main.dart`. Dimensions written as `px` in the portable frontmatter mean Flutter logical pixels, not CSS source values. Typography entries describe observed roles, not a replacement for Flutter's inherited text theme.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Pixel headings and mono metadata.
- Flat surfaces, tight photography, square/circle vault tiles.
- Black default appearance and warm cream light appearance.

Browser-preview review disposition: SHIP; all 11 review screenshots were valid with no material findings reported. This document directly inspected phone dark, phone light and desktop gallery captures. Native build acceptance, device performance and accessibility certification are not verified; full V2 is not complete.

## Colors

Rust is the primary accent family. Green is reserved for positive Keep feedback in swipe review (#64B58C on dark, #24694A on light). The frontmatter owns exact explicit source colors; remaining Material color roles are generated by `ColorScheme.fromSeed` and must come from the theme rather than guessed hex values.

### Primary
- Rust marks small status details. Theme primary uses a lighter rust in dark appearance and a deeper rust in light appearance for actions and selected navigation.
- Favorites has its own fixed rust fill. Memories uses a pale fixed fill with deeper rust labels in both appearances.

### Neutral
- Near-black and chalk are the dark background/text pair; cream and dark ink are the light pair.
- Containers and dividers change with appearance. White is also used for image overlays and Favorites content.

## Typography

DotGothic16 supplies headings; SpaceMono supplies body text, labels and actions. Bundled font families are used by Flutter; no CSS fallback stack is defined here.

Page headings use the display role. PixelTitle defaults to the title role and uses a line-height multiplier of 1.2. Gallery section headings and app-bar titles are 23 logical pixels; onboarding and desktop branding reach 44. Body copy commonly uses 13; explanatory copy and metadata commonly use 11, with explicit line heights of 1.8 or 1.9 where set. Other Material text sizes remain inherited.

## Layout

Page content generally has 24 logical pixels of horizontal inset. Main page headers use left/top 24, right 16 and bottom 14. Related controls use compact gaps; sections have more breathing room than the photo grid.

At viewport width 950 or above, a 220-wide sidebar replaces bottom navigation. Below that, four equally sized destinations sit in a SafeArea bottom bar. Navigation remains Gallery, Cleaner, Review, Insights.

Vault tiles wrap with 16 spacing and run spacing. Each side is half the available width minus 8, capped at 190. The photo grid uses square cells and a 2 gap: three columns by default, four when available grid width exceeds 550, six when it exceeds 800. The sliver grid computes this after subtracting its 48 horizontal inset. Filters scroll horizontally in a 52-high strip.

## Elevation & Depth

The gallery is flat. Tonal containers, thin dividers and photography separate regions rather than card shadows. Photo selection adds a black overlay at 30% opacity. White selection icons have a black shadow with blur radius 4 for legibility. Album covers have a transparent-to-black87 vertical gradient behind their labels. These are image treatments, not a general elevated-card system.

## Shapes

Favorites is a rounded square; Memories is a true circle, with radius half its side. Main gallery photo cells stay square. Non-sliver review grids clip their outer boundary at 20; album covers also use 20. Cleaner rows use 16 and thumbnail corners use 10. Shared buttons and fields use 14. Do not impose one radius on every surface.

## Components

### Buttons

Filled buttons have a minimum size of 48 by 52, horizontal padding 20 and vertical padding 14. Outlined buttons share the minimum size and corner treatment. Icon buttons have a 48 by 48 minimum. Colors and interaction states come from Flutter Material unless explicitly overridden. Cupertino dialogs and page routes keep confirmation and back navigation familiar.

### Inputs / Fields

Search uses a filled Material TextField on the theme container surface, with a 14 corner radius and no resting outline. Search prefix icon and placeholder describe date/category matching. Focus, error and disabled states are inherited rather than separately authored tokens.

### Chips

Gallery filters use ChoiceChip with 11-size labels, an 8 trailing gap and no checkmark. Selection and outlines come from the generated Material scheme. Do not copy screenshot-derived chip colors into new source tokens.

### Cards / Containers

The scan summary uses a theme container surface, 20 corners and 22 padding. Cleaner rows use the same surface with 16 corners/padding. Album cards use a 1.9 aspect ratio, photo cover, bottom gradient and white labels inset 20.

### Navigation

DotIcon draws each symbol on a 9 by 9 dot matrix, normally in a 26-square area. Active destinations use theme primary; others use onSurface. Bottom labels are 11 with a 6 gap below the icon. Desktop active rows gain a container fill. Semantic selected/button properties are present, but assistive-technology behavior still needs native testing.

### Vault and Photo Grid

Favorites and Memories center a 28 icon above 12-size titles and 11-size secondary labels. Preserve their fixed fills and geometry across appearance changes. Photo cells use cover crops, favorite hearts, video duration labels and explicit review selection indicators. Empty and loading states must remain visible when thumbnails or library access are unavailable.

### Swipe review

Review uses a three-card deck capped at 460 logical pixels wide. The card tracks a horizontal drag and rotates up to 0.2 radians. Its photo stays fully visible with contain fitting; metadata is inside the card. Noninteractive previews sit 10 and 20 pixels behind it.

A 28% width drag commits; a same-direction flick also commits after 30 pixels at over 700 pixels/second. Short drags return in 320 ms with easeOutBack; committed exits take 220 ms with easeInCubic. Keep and Queue stamps follow drag progress. Buttons share the gesture path. Reduce Motion removes tilt and timed flight. Decisions are persisted after the exit; a failed save returns the same card. Delete only queues an item.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- Do use DotGothic16 for headings and SpaceMono for labels and body text.
- Do keep the square Favorites and circular Memories silhouettes distinct.
- Do preserve visible sample-library labels and explicit deletion review.
- Do use the shared theme and actual widget dimensions when adding screens.

### Don't:
- Don't replace the palette with unrelated accents or generic gradients.
- Don't round every photo cell or turn the gallery into spaced cards.
- Don't present unavailable model or store features as working.
